# I﻿nspiration





L﻿essons learnt:

D﻿ifferent vers of different 'libraries' have different documentations and ways of describing things, sometimes when code screws up, it might just be an update issue.

S﻿ome programmes prioritise certain information from certain files, do pay notice to them.

U﻿se LLM's to condense large amount of documentation (for relevant ones) and as a search engiene across the web (They are your best friend)

S﻿omeone out there has already executed your idea to a certain degree, use it. Do not be afraid to use open source projects and build on it, never start coding from scratch (especially as a one man team). The internet is brimming with tools.

J﻿AMStack framework is king in web dev. ALWAYS have a CMS (they help a lot , making it easy for non-IT personnel to take part in content creation on whatever site), they also make it much easier for you to visuallise your content. Also, have a platform (like Netlify) such that pushes (or new entries) get immediately and automatically updated onto a live website, saving a lot of hassle in between.

W﻿hy I did not use WIX (or other web dev platforms):

1﻿. Too expensive (monthly fees not worth it)

2﻿. Only limited to their ecosystem shifting a website out of this ecosystem is almost impossible 

3﻿. Inflexible. (I can control where I want what, or what softwares I want to showcase, even within the website because I own my code)
