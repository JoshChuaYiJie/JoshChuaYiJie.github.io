# O﻿ne word: Nuts

W﻿hat happens when you group 33 boys together, make them work with one another 24/7 5-6 days a week to carry out insane missions? You get havoc, you get fights, you get arguments. But you also get solidarity, brotherhood and an experience like no other. That basically sums up my whole experience.

F﻿rom the incessant rain of Brunei to the arid lands of Thailand to the mosquito filled Tekong, we've been through it all. We've went through days on end with no food, carried loads half of our bodyweight while running, and even got lost in the woods together. But together with those days were nights around a campfire cooking maggie or warming ourselves, there were nights where we spooned each other to sleep, nights where we spent hours talking while 'doing' sentry duty,

I﻿n this entry, I'll recap everything that happened in the 9 months, basically as a goodbye to these experiences (and also since it's a good read).

# S﻿ervice term (first 3 months)

B﻿asically a good slap to the face, 'Welcome to ECHO'. 

## I﻿FM

I﻿nterval fast march, a weekly tradition where we put heavy stuff in our bags and breeze walked. Echo being the tryhard wing it was, made us do extra IFMs. I remember I was always at the back with the last place gang and when one set was done everyone would flop on the ground and eat an ice pop.
